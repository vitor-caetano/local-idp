# Phase 1: GitOps and traffic

**Goal:** ArgoCD reconciling the foundation from Git, with every UI reachable over HTTPS at
`*.localtest.me`.

**Inputs:** The cluster from phase 0.

**Outputs:**
- `platform/` created (from `solution/platform/` or generated by your agent)
- `./provision/set-git-source.sh gitea` run and committed; `grep -rn --include="*.yaml" REPLACE_WITH platform/` empty
- ArgoCD installed by `./provision/bootstrap-argocd.sh` with `platform/0-bootstrap/argocd-values.yaml`
- The Backstage image built and pushed by `./images/backstage/build-and-push.sh` **before** seeding.
  Seeding starts every wave; without the image, Backstage (wave 5) sits in ImagePullBackOff and holds
  wave 6 (routes and the policy baseline) with it
- Gitea installed, seeded with your tree, and the foundation App-of-Apps applied by
  `./provision/seed-gitea.sh`
- Wave 0 to 2 components Synced and Healthy: `platform-namespaces`, `cert-manager`,
  `envoy-gateway`, `gitea`, `cert-manager-issuers`, `platform-gateway`
- The `platform-gateway` Gateway Programmed, with a wildcard certificate from the `local-idp-ca`
  ClusterIssuer

Later waves keep syncing in the background. Their tests belong to later phases.

**Test criteria (`tests/test_phase_1_gitops_and_traffic.py`):**
- ArgoCD pods Ready; `platform-foundation` exists and is Synced
- The six Applications above are Synced and Healthy
- `ClusterIssuer/local-idp-ca` Ready; `Certificate/wildcard-localtest-me` Ready
- `Gateway/platform-gateway` Programmed; the Envoy Service is a NodePort on 30080 and 30443
- From the host, `http://gitea.localtest.me` answers 301 to https (the redirect route works through
  the NodePort mapping, with no dependency on later waves)

**Completion promise:** `<promise>PHASE1_DONE</promise>`

**Key decisions:**
- Envoy Gateway implements Gateway API; ingress-nginx is end of life.
- Kind has no load balancer. The EnvoyProxy resource makes the data plane a NodePort pinned to
  30080/30443, which `kind-config.yaml` maps to host 80/443.
- The root CA is generated in the cluster by cert-manager. `./provision/trust-ca.sh` exports it.
- The `argoproj.io_Application` health check makes sync waves wait for health, so a stuck early
  wave holds later ones. That is where to look first.

**Try the switch:** `./provision/set-git-source.sh github https://github.com/<you>/local-idp.git`,
push to GitHub, re-apply `root-app.yaml`, and watch every Application change source. Switch back
with `set-git-source.sh gitea` and `push-to-cluster.sh`.

**Stop here.**
